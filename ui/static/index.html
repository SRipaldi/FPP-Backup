      Live show
      
      
      
        
        
        
      
      
    
      
    
      Every 3 seconds: what the player is playing, and whether each remote is playing the same sequence at the same point and receiving the player's sync packets. Positions are whole seconds (that is what FPP reports), so a 1 s difference is normal. Read-only.
      
    
      
    
      
  
      

  
      
    
      
      
      ColorLight
      
      
      
    
      
    
      Every controller that drives a ColorLight receiver card, checked from its own logs every 5 minutes while the app runs: link drops on the card's network port, relinks at 100 Mbps (the card needs gigabit), whether fppd found the card when it last started, card restarts, and slow or failed sends. History is kept for 14 days. Read-only.
